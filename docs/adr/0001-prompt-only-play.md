# Prompt-only play

The player never controls a character directly: they clear each Level by writing a Prompt, then watching the Companion act it out until the Attempt succeeds or fails. We chose this over a sidekick design (the player platforms while prompting the Companion for help) because LLM replies take seconds and differ from run to run; a prompt-then-watch loop absorbs both, and it makes the Prompt Budget the puzzle itself rather than a mana bar on top of an ordinary platformer.
